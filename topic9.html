Technology Overview

Digital services, startups and innovation

Pakistan's technology sector includes software development, IT-enabled services, digital platforms, freelancing, startups and technology-based businesses.

Major cities such as Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad-Rawalpindi have developed significant technology and startup ecosystems.
