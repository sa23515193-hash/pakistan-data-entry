Infrastructure Overview

Transportation, connectivity and economic infrastructure

Infrastructure connects communities, businesses and markets. Pakistan's transportation system includes roads, highways, railways, airports and seaports.

Efficient transport infrastructure supports domestic mobility, tourism, agriculture, industry and international trade.
