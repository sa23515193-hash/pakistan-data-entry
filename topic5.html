Population Overview

Understanding Pakistan's demographic structure

Pakistan has a large and diverse population distributed across provinces and administrative regions. Population statistics are important for planning education, healthcare, employment, housing, transportation and other public services.

Punjab represents the largest population concentration, followed by Sindh and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Urbanization is also changing the distribution of population across major cities.
