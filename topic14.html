Demographic Overview

Population distribution and demographic structure

Demographic statistics help describe the size, distribution and characteristics of a population. They are useful for planning education, healthcare, housing, employment and infrastructure.

Pakistan's population is distributed differently across provinces and between urban and rural areas. Demographic patterns also change over time because of migration, urbanization and population growth.
