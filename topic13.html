Energy Overview

Generation, consumption and energy sources

Pakistan's energy system includes thermal, hydropower, nuclear and renewable sources. Reliable energy is important for households, businesses, agriculture and industry.

Renewable energy technologies such as solar and wind are increasingly relevant to the country's long-term energy development.
