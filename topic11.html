Economic Overview

Agriculture, industry, services and international trade

Pakistan's economy consists of agriculture, industry and services. These sectors contribute to employment, production, domestic activity and international trade.

International trade includes exports and imports of goods and services. Textile products, agricultural commodities, information technology and other products contribute to Pakistan's trade activity.
