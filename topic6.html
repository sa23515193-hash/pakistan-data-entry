Healthcare Overview

Healthcare infrastructure and human resources

Healthcare is an essential component of national development. Pakistan's healthcare system includes public hospitals, private healthcare facilities, clinics and specialized medical institutions.

Access to healthcare differs between urban and rural areas. Health workforce availability, hospital infrastructure and preventive healthcare remain important areas of planning.
