Industrial Overview

Manufacturing, industries and international trade

Pakistan's industrial sector includes textiles, food processing, sports goods, leather, chemicals, engineering products and other manufacturing activities.

Exports are an important source of foreign exchange. Textile products represent a major category of Pakistan's international merchandise exports.
