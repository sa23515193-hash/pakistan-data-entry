Agricultural Overview

Crops, farming and food production

Agriculture remains an important sector of Pakistan's economy and supports rural livelihoods, food production and agricultural industries.

Major crops include wheat, rice, sugarcane and cotton. Production varies by province because of differences in climate, irrigation, soil and agricultural practices.
